## 1. Introduction

Your privacy is our top priority. Quiet Translator is designed from the ground up
as a zero-retention application. Voice input is processed in real time and displayed
on screen — nothing is recorded, saved, or stored anywhere.

This Privacy Policy explains exactly what data we collect, why, and what rights you have.

---

## 2. Microphone Access and Audio Data

Quiet Translator requires access to your device microphone solely to provide
real-time speech translation.

Your audio is streamed securely over HTTPS to the following AI service providers:

| Provider | Purpose |
|---|---|
| OpenAI (Whisper) | Speech transcription |
| Deepgram | Real-time transcription |
| AssemblyAI | Real-time transcription |
| DeepL via AWS Serverless Proxy | Text translation |

**We do NOT save, record, or store any audio files —
on your device or on our servers.**

Audio is streamed in real time and immediately discarded after processing.
Server-side audio retention: **0 days.**

---

## 3. No Transcript Storage

Translated text is displayed on screen during your active session only.
It is not stored in any database, log, or file after the session ends.

Server-side transcript retention: **0 days.**
On-device transcript retention: **0 days.**

---

## 4. Metadata We Collect

For billing, analytics, and account management we collect only
the following anonymized metadata:

- User ID (pseudonymous internal identifier)
- Language pairs used (e.g., French → English)
- Session duration in seconds
- API character counts (for billing purposes)
- Device OS and app version (for crash diagnostics)

We do **not** collect the content of your conversations.

**Account data:** your email address and hashed password,
stored solely for authentication and subscription management.

**Metadata retention:** 24 months from last activity,
then automatic permanent deletion.

information we collect, the right to delete it, and the right to opt out of the

Submit requests to: privacy@[yourdomain.com]
