Delete Account

Effective // June 20, 2026 // Last Updated: June 20, 2026 // NeuroOrbit AI

TL;DR: You can delete your account and all associated data at any time — directly in the app in a few taps, or by email. There is no recovery after deletion.

// 01 — Recommended

Delete Your Account In-App

The fastest way to permanently delete your account is directly inside Quiet Translator:

  1. Open Quiet Translator and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account at the bottom of the screen.
  4. Confirm your identity when prompted (sign in again with Apple, Google, or email).
  5. Your account and all associated data are permanently deleted, and you are signed out. You will see the confirmation “Your account has been deleted.”

If you signed in with Sign in with Apple, your Apple authorization token is also revoked as part of the deletion, so the app is removed from your Apple ID.

// 02 — Alternative

Request Deletion by Email

If you are unable to access the app, you can request deletion by email instead.

Send an email to mission@neuroorbitai.com with:

  • Subject: Account Deletion Request
  • Your registered email address
  • The text: “I request permanent deletion of my account and all associated data.”

We will process your request within 30 days.
