// CONTEXT


Industrial Noise Kills Communication

Heavy machinery, ambient hum, and crowded factory floors make team communication nearly impossible. Standard translation apps fail — their microphone configs can't isolate voice, and sentence-by-sentence delays are unacceptable on the production floor.

A Two-Phase Audio Pipeline

Quiet Translator uses forced microphone routing and an Environment Noise Filter to cut through background interference. Speak into the main mic, get translated audio directly in your earpiece — without distracting the people around you.
