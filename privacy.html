Privacy Policy

Effective // January 1, 2025 // Last Updated: May 2, 2026 // NeuroOrbit AI

TL;DR: We do not store your audio. We do not store your translations. Your conversations are yours alone. We collect only what is necessary to run the app — your email for login and anonymous session metadata for billing.

// 01

Data Controller

The data controller is NeuroOrbit AI, Montreal, Quebec, Canada.
Privacy contact: mission@neuroorbitai.com

EU Representative (GDPR Art. 27): To be designated prior to EU public release.

// 02

No Audio Storage

Voice input is streamed in real time to our AI processing providers and is immediately discarded upon completion of the translation request.

We DO NOT save, record, or store any audio files — on your device or on our servers. Server-side audio retention: 0 days.

Your audio is streamed securely over HTTPS to the following AI service providers:

ProviderPurpose
OpenAI (Whisper)Speech transcription
DeepgramReal-time transcription
AssemblyAIReal-time transcription
DeepL (via AWS Serverless Proxy)Text translation

None of these providers retain your audio after processing.

// 03

No Transcript Storage

Translated text is displayed on screen during your active session only. It is not stored in any database, log, or file after the session ends.

Server-side transcript retention: 0 days. On-device transcript retention: 0 days.

// 04

Data We Collect

For billing, analytics, and account management we collect only the following anonymized metadata:

  • User ID — pseudonymous internal identifier
  • Language pairs used — e.g., French to English
  • Session duration — in seconds
  • API character counts — for billing purposes
  • Device OS and app version — for crash diagnostics

We do not collect the content of your conversations.

Account data: your email address and hashed password, stored solely for authentication and subscription management.

Metadata retention: 24 months from last activity, then automatic permanent deletion.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Erase your data — right to be forgotten
  • Port your data to another service
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior lawful processing

California residents (CCPA/CPRA): You have the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to delete it, and the right to opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information.

Submit requests to: mission@neuroorbitai.com
We respond within 30 days (45 days for CCPA requests).

// 07

International Data Transfers

Data may be processed on servers located in the United States and other countries.

  • EU/EEA and UK users: transfers are carried out under Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) adopted by the European Commission (Decision 2021/914)

// 08

// 09
